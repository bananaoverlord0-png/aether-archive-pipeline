# Documentary Script Structure (Aether Archive)

Target runtime: **8–15 minutes**  
Narration: **~1,200–2,200 words** (~140 wpm)  
Language: English  
Tone: mysterious, intelligent, suspenseful, accessible

## Sections

### A. Cold open
Compelling question, surprising **verified** fact, or unresolved contradiction.  
Avoid misleading clickbait. 80–150 words.

### B. Context
Location, historical period, scientific background, or original discovery. 150–250 words.

### C. The mystery
Central question and why it matters. 100–180 words.

### D. Evidence
Strongest documented facts, records, discoveries, observations.  
Map every claim to claim IDs with statuses. 250–400 words.

### E. Investigation
Competing explanations, theories, weaknesses. Preserve disagreement. 250–400 words.

### F. Turning point
Important discovery, contradiction, or shift in understanding. 120–200 words.

### G. Synthesis
What can be said with confidence; what remains open. 150–250 words.

### H. Close
Honest, satisfying conclusion. Invite curiosity without false certainty. CTA. 80–150 words.

## Labels (mandatory)

- **[VERIFIED]** — multi-source high reliability  
- **[PARTIALLY VERIFIED]** — supported but incomplete  
- **[DISPUTED]** — reputable sources disagree  
- **[THEORY]** — explanatory model, not established fact  
- **[FOLKLORE]** — tradition without primary evidence  
- **[RECONSTRUCTION]** — dramatized or modeled visualization  

## Forbidden

- Fabricated sources, quotes, dates, or eyewitness accounts  
- Presenting speculation as fact  
- Copying another creator’s distinctive scripts, thumbs, or branding  
- Instructional criminal methods  

## Visual & SFX cues

Each section ends with `visual_cues` and `sfx_cues` lists used by visual_engine and editor_engine.
